The Resume

Built the Hard Way

Jon Sioredas is the Head Wrestling Coach at Cal Poly, where he just led the most decorated season in program history: six All Americans and a third place NCAA finish by Zeth Romney headlining a breakthrough year on the national stage.

Beyond the dual meets and the podiums, Jon runs the full operation. He directs Mustang Wrestling Camp, leads a staff of seven, and partners with a donor foundation to fund and grow the program. He knows what it takes to build, because he has done the building.

That is what he brings to your room: not just technique, but the standards and systems behind a program on the rise.
