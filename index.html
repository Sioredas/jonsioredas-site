The Turnaround

The Turnaround

Last Placeto Top 25

When Jon arrived in San Luis Obispo before the 2016-17 season, Cal Poly had just finished last in the NCAA and had no NCAA qualifiers. On his first day he wrote down a single goal: a Top 25 program within five years.

The Mustangs lost every dual in his second and third seasons. Instead of chasing quick fixes, he rebuilt the foundation: launching the Mustang Wrestling Foundation, overhauling fundraising and anchoring the program to a set of core values.

  1. 2016Day OneLast in the NCAA. No NCAA qualifiers. One goal: Top 25 in five years.
  2. 2018The FoundationThe Mustang Wrestling Foundation launches and the rebuild takes root.
  3. 2021Goal Reached25th in the nation. The Mustangs have not left the Top 25 since.
  4. 2022Top 10Into the national top 10 for the first time in 39 years.
  5. 2023Conference ChampionsA 5-0 run to the first Pac-12 dual title in program history and Pac-12 Coach of the Year.
  6. 2025NCAA PodiumThird place at the NCAA Championships at 133 pounds and a Top 15 national ranking.
  7. 2026Record SeasonThree Pac-12 champions, a second Coach of the Year award and the NWCA presidency.

For Host Coaches

How Hosts Fill the Room

Most programs do not pay for a day like this out of the budget. They build an event around it, and the event pays for the day.

  • Open the doorsInvite the programs around you. Four or five teams in one room turns a clinic into the best day of the off season for everybody in it.
  • Charge at the doorHosts set their own per wrestler entry. The day covers itself, and what is left over funds your program.
  • Split a dateTwo or three programs can co host and share one day, one travel trip, one room full of wrestlers.

About Jon

A CompetitorFirst

Before he built programs, Jon won at every level.

At Great Bridge High School in Chesapeake, Virginia, he was a state champion, national champion and All-American who went 181-13, the fourth-most wins in Virginia high school history. At Chattanooga he placed fifth at 165 pounds at the 2005 NCAA Championships to become an All-American, won two Southern Conference titles and was named SoCon Wrestler of the Year and tournament MVP. He was later inducted into the UTC Athletics Hall of Fame.

In 20 seasons of college coaching, 12 of them as a head coach, he has been part of 11 nationally ranked teams and has coached 10 NCAA All-Americans, 70 NCAA qualifiers and 24 Academic All-Americans, while recruiting seven Top 25 classes.
